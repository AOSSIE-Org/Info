# AOSSIE's Idea List for Google Summer of Code 2027

**Attention: do not spam our repos with unsolicited AI-generated issues and PRs!**

As an umbrella organization since its inception 11 years ago, AOSSIE has many projects and many ideas for new projects. Until recently, we have tried to maintain a uniform GSoC selection procedure for all our projects. We asked GSoC applicants to contribute PRs to our repos, and the selection criteria emphasized the number and quality of PRs. This approach was not unique to AOSSIE. Many GSoC orgs had similar approaches resulting from an evolutionary process over time. It is an approach that was found to work in practice. 

However, with the popularization of AI for generation of code, this approach became vulnerable to floods of issues and PRs in the past two years. This caused an excess of maintenance overhead. A significant amount of time had to be spent triaging and closing duplicate and low-value issues and PRs.

Clearly a new approach is needed. But the best approach is not known yet. And it may well be that there is no single approach that fits all orgs and all projects anymore. Therefore, this year, we will let each idea set its own procedure and criteria, according to its needs.

The key principle stays the same: we want contributions and we want the application phase to be valuable for you, for AOSSIE and for open-source software in general, independently of whether you succeed in getting a spot in GSoC.
